How can we help?

Genie Chatbot lets users interact with Databricks Genie AI agents from Power BI.

Getting started

  1. Add Genie Chatbot to your Power BI report.
  2. Ensure your organization has configured the required backend services and access.
  3. Complete the authentication flow provided by your organization's Power BI environment.
  4. Enter a question and submit it to the configured Databricks Genie agent.

Your organization's administrator may need to configure access to the chatbot backend and Databricks Genie before the visual can be used.

Common support topics

  • Installing or adding the visual to a report
  • Authentication and single sign-on issues
  • Connection issues with the chatbot service
  • Questions not receiving a response
  • Unexpected visual behaviour or errors
