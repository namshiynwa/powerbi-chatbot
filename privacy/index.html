1. About Genie Chatbot

Genie Chatbot is a Power BI custom visual that enables users to submit natural-language questions to Databricks Genie AI agents from within Power BI. The visual communicates with backend services configured by the organization deploying it.

2. Information that may be processed

  • Authentication information: Microsoft Entra ID tokens or relevant identity claims used for authentication and authorization.
  • User prompts: Questions and text submitted through the chatbot.
  • Conversation information: Conversation IDs, message IDs, responses, and conversation history where enabled by the backend.
  • Technical information: Request timestamps, error details, diagnostic information, and service logs.
