Privacy Policy

This policy explains how QBurst processes information when you use the Genie Chatbot Power BI custom visual.

About Genie Chatbot

Genie Chatbot is a Power BI custom visual that enables users to submit natural-language questions to Databricks Genie AI agents from within Power BI. The visual communicates with backend services configured by the organization deploying it.

Information that may be processed

  • Authentication information: Microsoft Entra ID tokens or relevant identity claims used for authentication and authorization.
  • User prompts: Questions and text submitted through the chatbot.
  • Conversation information: Conversation IDs, message IDs, responses, and conversation history where enabled by the backend.

How information is used

Information may be used to authenticate users, enforce access controls, transmit questions to the configured Databricks Genie agent, return responses to Power BI.

Services involved

Depending on deployment, information may be transmitted between Microsoft Power BI and Microsoft Entra ID for reporting and authentication, Microsoft Azure (including the configured Azure Function App) to process chatbot requests, and Databricks (including Databricks Genie) to process questions and generate responses. These providers are subject to their applicable terms, privacy statements, and the agreements/configuration established by the organization operating the service.

Storage and retention

The Genie Chatbot Power BI visual itself does not store any data, either locally (for example, in the browser or on the user's device) or in the cloud. Prompts, responses, and conversation information are only held in memory while the visual is in use.
